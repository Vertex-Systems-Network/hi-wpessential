# Saffron — restaurant demo (Pro)

**Brand:** Saffron — modern South-Asian kitchen & bar, two locations.
**Job of the site:** get bookings and online orders; show the menu fast on mobile.

## Research (Oct 2026)
| Reference | Sections they share | We take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Envato restaurant top sellers (Grand Restaurant, Chow, La Food, Neve restaurant demo) | Hero with "Book a table", featured dishes, menu by category (starters/mains/desserts/drinks), chef story, gallery, testimonials, opening hours, reservation form | All of these |
| Real restaurants (Dishoom, Gymkhana, Nando's) | Locations with hours + map, online ordering, private dining / events, gift cards, allergens note, newsletter | Locations, Order online (WooCommerce), Private dining, Gift card |
| 2026 guides (Barn2, WPArena, Gutenverse) | Clear menu sections, reservation CTA in header, block-theme editing | Sticky "Book a table" button, menu tabs |

Sources: barn2.com/wordpress-restaurant-themes, wparena.com/wordpress-restaurant-themes, gutenverse.com/best-restaurant-wordpress-theme-for-2026, templatemonster.com.

## Pages
Home · Menu · Reservations · Order online (shop) · Our story · Private dining & events · Gallery · Locations & contact

## Direction
- Palette: charcoal #1A1412 ink, saffron #E8A33D primary, chilli #B23A2B accent, cream #FBF5EC surface, warm white.
- Type: Playfair Display (display) + DM Sans (UI).
- Dark, moody food photography; rounded 12 px; menu lists with dotted price leaders.
- Plugins: WooCommerce (order online), WPForms (reservations, events enquiry).
