# Business Pro — corporate group demo (Pro)

**Brand:** Corvane Group — a multi-industry corporate group (energy, logistics, real estate, finance).
**Job of the site:** build trust with clients, investors and job seekers; route each to the right page.

## Direction
- Palette: navy #0B1F3A ink, steel blue #1F5EFF primary, sand #F4F1EA surface-alt, white surface, gold #C8A24A accent.
- Type: Manrope (display 800) + Inter (UI).
- Radius 6 px, thin borders, wide 1280 container, generous numbers.
- Differentiation vs Base (Northwind): corporate group (not consultancy), investor & sustainability content, darker navy palette.
