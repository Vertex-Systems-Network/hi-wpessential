## 2. What all of them have

**Home page**

1. Header with a phone or email line and a "Get a consultation" button
2. Hero: one clear promise plus a CTA. Split hero (text with image or video) or a video background.
3. Client logo strip
4. Services overview (6 cards, each linking to its own page)
5. About block with numbers (years, clients, projects, satisfaction)
6. How we work (3–4 steps)
7. Case studies or projects with results ("+62% leads")
8. Testimonials (carousel with photo, name, company)
9. Team preview
10. Pricing or a cost calculator (Avantage), optional
11. FAQ
12. Latest news (3 posts)
13. Big CTA band ("Book a free consultation")
14. Footer: offices, hours, newsletter, socials

**Service page** (one per service): what it is, who it is for, what is included, outcomes, process, related case study, FAQ, a CTA with a short form.

**About page:** story, mission and values, numbers, timeline, team, awards and partners, careers, CTA.

**Case studies:** filterable grid. Each single page covers challenge → solution → results (stats) → client quote.

**Contact page:** offices with a map, phone, email and hours, a short form (name, email, company, need, message), "book a call", FAQ.

**Extra pages most demos ship:** Team / single team member, Pricing, FAQ, Careers, Blog and single post, 404, Coming soon.

## 3. What our current Base demo gets wrong

Our Base demo is designed as **a site selling a WordPress theme**: "40+ demos", "Gutenberg or Elementor", a demo library. A customer who imports "Business" expects **a business or consulting company site**. That is the main reason it does not feel right.

| Must have | Base now |
|---|---|
| Business hero with a consultation CTA | ❌ theme-marketing hero |
| Client logos | ✅ |
| Services with their own pages | ⚠️ 6 cards, no single service pages |
| About with numbers | ⚠️ on About only |
| Process | ⚠️ on Services only |
| Case studies with results | ❌ |
| Testimonials | ✅ |
| Team | ⚠️ About only, no single member page |
| Pricing / calculator | ⚠️ theme plans (Free/Pro/Agency), not business pricing |
| FAQ | ✅ |
| News | ✅ |
| CTA band | ✅ |
| Contact with map, offices, hours | ⚠️ no map or offices |
| Careers, Case study single, Team single | ❌ |

## 4. Proposed new Base demo: "Northwind Advisory" (business consulting)

The same structure works for agencies, finance and IT services, so it stays a good base demo.

**Pages:** Home, About, Services, 6 single service pages, Case studies, single case study, Team, Pricing, FAQ, Careers, Blog, single post, Contact, 404.

**Home section order:** top bar (phone, email, hours) → header with a "Free consultation" button → split hero (promise, 2 CTAs, photo, rating badge) → client logos → services (6) → about with 4 numbers → process (4 steps) → case studies (3 with results) → testimonials → pricing (3 packages) → FAQ → news → CTA band → footer with offices.

**Forms (WPForms):** consultation request on Contact and Services, a newsletter, and a careers application.
