# Campus — online courses / LMS demo (Pro)

**Brand:** Campus — online academy for design, code and business skills.
**Job of the site:** sell courses and memberships; help students find and continue courses.

## Research (Oct 2026)
| Reference | Sections they share | We take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eduma (47k+ sales), MasterStudy, Academist (Envato) | Search hero, course categories, popular courses grid, instructors, stats, testimonials, pricing/membership, events, blog | All of these |
| Coursera, Udemy, Skillshare, Domestika | Course filters (level, duration, price), course page with curriculum accordion, instructor box, reviews, sticky enrol card, student dashboard with progress | Catalog with filters, course detail, dashboard |
| Free LMS themes (Astra + LMS, Prespa) | LearnPress/Tutor LMS compatibility | LearnPress-ready layouts |

Sources: themesgrove.com/resources/best-education-wordpress-themes, colorlib.com/wp/lms-wordpress-themes, competethemes.com/blog/education-wordpress-themes.

## Pages
Home · Courses · Course detail · Instructors · Instructor profile · Pricing (membership) · Student dashboard · Become an instructor · About · Contact

## Direction
- Palette: deep indigo #1E1B4B ink, violet #6D4AFF primary, mint #22C55E success, lemon #FDE68A highlight, #F7F7FB surface-alt.
- Type: Outfit (display) + Inter (UI).
- Friendly, rounded 16 px cards, progress bars, rating stars, level badges.
- Plugins: LearnPress (courses), WooCommerce (payments), WPForms (contact, instructor application).
