# Demo design rules (all demos)

Owner decision, Oct 2026.

1. **No demo is minimalist.** Every demo must look premium and art-directed:
   - full-bleed photo heroes with gradient overlays, large tight display type (72–104 px desktop);
   - layered sections (dark / light / tinted), bento and asymmetric layouts, not only equal card grids;
   - depth: soft shadows, overlapping media, glass stat bars, pill buttons with arrows;
   - real photography in every section; numbers, proof and social signals on the home page;
   - generous spacing (140–150 px section padding desktop) and a big brand footer.
2. **"Minimal" is its own niche.** A dedicated minimalist demo (e.g. a portfolio/studio) may be added
   as a separate product; its restraint must not leak into other demos.
3. Existing demos (Base/Northwind, Maison, Pulse, WPEssential.org) get re-checked against these rules
   and upgraded where they look plain.
4. New demos (Business Pro, Saffron, Campus) are designed to these rules from the start.
5. **Every demo's Contact (or Locations) page must include a Google map** of the business address,
   in Figma and in both builders (Gutenberg iframe `.demo-map`, Elementor `google_maps` widget).
